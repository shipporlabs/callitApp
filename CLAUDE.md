## 1. Quick Context for Claude Code / Codex

Callit bridges **Solana prediction markets** (via Panta) with **NEAR Protocol's Chain Abstraction** and **Illia Polosukhin's AI Truth-Seeking Thesis**:

1. **NEAR 1-Click Cross-Chain Intents (Funding Rail)**: Eliminates multi-chain bridging. Users holding assets on NEAR (`wNEAR`, `NEAR`), Ethereum (`ETH`), Base, or Arbitrum can fund their Callit Solana balance in 1 click using the decentralized Defuse Intent Solver network.
2. **AI Truth Consensus & Living Encyclopedia (Illia's Thesis)**: Implements NEAR co-founder Illia Polosukhin's thesis: *"Instead of community curation, every fact becomes a perpetual truth-seeking market (Augur-style). The official pages are generated by AI based on current market understanding."* Real-time market odds and capital allocation dynamically generate objective, living encyclopedia entries with verifiable evidence drivers.
3. **Fiat On-Ramp**: Virtual Nigerian Naira (NGN) bank transfer on-ramp via FossaPay.
4. **AI Market Creation**: Natural language takes (tweets, news headlines) are compiled into structured prediction markets using Groq LLaMA-3.3 70B with an automated local heuristic fallback compiler.

### 3.3 "What did you build?" (`idea` field text — 1,745 / 2,000 chars)

```text
Callit is a mobile-first social prediction market platform that pioneers two foundational NEAR ecosystem innovations: 1-Click Cross-Chain Intent Funding and a Perpetual AI Living Truth Encyclopedia.

Prediction markets represent the purest mechanism for discovering truth, but they face two massive bottlenecks: multi-chain liquidity fragmentation and raw odds that lack objective contextualization. Callit solves both.

First, Callit integrates the NEAR Intents protocol (Defuse open solver network). Callit's execution layer operates on Solana via Panta, but users holding assets on NEAR (wNEAR, NEAR), Ethereum, Base, or Arbitrum no longer need to navigate cumbersome bridges, wrapped assets, or high slippage. With NEAR Intents, users specify their deposit intent in 1 click; decentralized solvers execute the swap and credit the user's Solana wallet with native SOL/USDC seamlessly.

Second, Callit brings NEAR co-founder Illia Polosukhin's thesis to life: "AI encyclopedia with prediction markets: instead of community curation, every fact becomes a perpetual truth-seeking market. The official pages are generated by AI based on current market understanding." For every market, Callit dynamically analyzes live odds, volume, and verified resolution rules to synthesize a living encyclopedia entry. Users don't just see numbers—they read an objective, market-derived reality assessment with key evidence drivers.

Under the hood:
- Go backend deployed with PM2 on VPS, featuring title-enrichment caching, resilient Groq LLaMA-3.3 70B AI compilers, and NEAR Intent quote/status endpoints.
- Flutter iOS & Android client with tactile neo-brutalist styling, 3-tier deposit modal (Naira Bank Transfer, Solana Crypto, NEAR Intents), and full-screen Truth Consensus encyclopedia sheets.
```
